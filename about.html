About Aviate

Aviate signifies the beginning.

What began as a dream project has grown into a mission: help students begin the career of their dreams, and help partner agencies grow alongside them.

Our story

Aviate your career

Aviate has spent over a decade helping students choose the right career path, garnering trust one placement at a time. Every recommendation runs through a single question: is this genuinely the right course, college and country for this student?

That same discipline now underpins our partnership with KC Overseas Education — extending Aviate's counselling approach across 45+ countries and 1,200+ universities, supported by the coursefinder.ai platform.

Our vision

To become the world leader in connecting students and universities.

Our mission

Helping students find and apply to the right course, right college and right country — in just three clicks.

Our framework

The 3C Model

Every student profile, every course search and every application inside coursefinder.ai is built around three questions, answered in order.

C

Right Course

Matched to marks, interests and career goals — not just what's available.

C

Right College

Weighed against recognition, faculty strength and clinical or industry exposure.

C

Right Country

Balanced against budget, visa pathway and long‑term career plans.
