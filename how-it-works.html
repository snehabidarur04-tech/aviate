How It Works

Three steps from first conversation to first enrolment.

Becoming a recruitment partner with Aviate and KC doesn't require new infrastructure — just a signed partnership and access to the platform.

01

Partner with Aviate + KC

Complete a short partnership enquiry, and our team verifies your agency and sets up your coursefinder.ai access — usually within a few working days. No onboarding fee, no minimum volume commitment.

02

Recruit students globally

Use the platform to match students to the right course, college and country, submit applications with the correct documentation the first time, and track visa progress through to decision.

03

Grow your business

Receive on‑time commissions per enrolment, access an expanding list of represented universities, and lean on 360° support as your student volume grows.

What partners get at every step

Support that runs the whole distance

Admissions

Student profiling against the 3C Model, documentation guidance and interview preparation handled by a dedicated admissions team.

Visa

Financial documentation, medical papers and interview prep begin up to nine months ahead of the college session for a safety cushion.

Pre‑departure

Flight booking, accommodation, insurance, SIM cards and airport pickup — a fully planned transit, not a checklist left to the student.
